# What AI assistants answer when asked which brand to buy

**AI Answers Atlas, edition 1, October 2026. Full edition in French.**

We asked ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity 60 buying questions, in French, across three
categories where French brands sell online: mattresses, clean beauty and comfort shoes. We then
asked 12 of those questions four more times to each assistant, to see what holds and what
changes from one answer to the next. 432 answers in total, each recorded, counted and coded. Every question was asked by
hand in the consumer apps, so these are the answers a shopper actually sees, product cards and
sources included.

## Five findings {#findings}

**1. AI assistants almost always answer with brand names.** 190 of the 192 repeated answers name
at least one brand; the other two are generic advice and a refusal. A brand that is not in the
answer is not left out of a neutral reply: its place goes to someone else.

**2. The same question does not get the same answer twice.** Asked four times in the same week,
by the same assistant, fewer than one cited brand in five came back every time (128 of 690), and
nearly half appeared only once (337 of 690). Checking a question once in ChatGPT says little; the
only reliable measure is a frequency over several tries.

**3. The four assistants do not recommend the same brands.** In 23 of the 40 cases where a brand
we track is cited consistently by one assistant (at least 3 answers out of 4), another assistant
never cites it on the same question. Visibility in ChatGPT says nothing about Gemini or
Perplexity.

**4. Depending on the category, the assistants rely on different sources.** Across the sources
ChatGPT and Perplexity cite on buying questions, the press and specialist sites (reviews, buying
guides, blogs) account for 378 of 706 classified citations. The mix changes completely by
category: press and review sites make up 200 of 266 citations for mattresses, while shops and
manufacturers' sites make up 133 of 206 for comfort shoes, where health sites also appear.
Customer review platforms are almost never cited on a buying question.

**5. In each category, one to three brands appear in more than half of the answers.** Tediber,
Emma and Hypnia for mattresses; Patyka and Typology for clean beauty; Podowell for comfort shoes.
Behind them the field is wide open: between 43 and 111 different brands are cited per category.

## Scope {#scope}

Three categories and 60 questions, asked in French on logged-in accounts with memory turned off,
on 25 September and from 5 to 8 October 2026. AI answers change with time and model updates. The
method, the limits and the aggregated data are on the French pages.
